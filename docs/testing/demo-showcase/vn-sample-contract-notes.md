# Redline VN Demo Showcase - Sample Contract Notes

Status: lightweight Vietnamese showcase source, not a rigorous eval set.

Use this file to generate local DOCX fixtures for a Vietnamese demo path. The content is intentionally compact and clause-focused so the demo can show parser truth, deterministic compare, RAG AI Review, and Contract Q&A without spending time on long legal drafting.

Generated DOCX files belong under `output/demo-showcase/fixtures/` and should not be committed.

## VN NDA v1 - Thoa thuan bao mat

### 1. Muc dich va pham vi su dung

Ben Nhan chi duoc su dung Thong Tin Bao Mat de danh gia co hoi hop tac voi Ben Tiet Lo. Ben Nhan khong duoc chia se Thong Tin Bao Mat cho ben thu ba neu chua co chap thuan bang van ban cua Ben Tiet Lo.

### 2. Ngoai le doi voi Thong Tin Bao Mat

Thong Tin Bao Mat khong bao gom thong tin da duoc Ben Nhan biet hop phap truoc khi cong bo, thong tin tro thanh cong khai khong do loi cua Ben Nhan, thong tin duoc phat trien doc lap ma khong su dung Thong Tin Bao Mat, hoac thong tin nhan duoc hop phap tu ben thu ba khong bi rang buoc bao mat.

### 3. Thoi han bao mat

Nghia vu bao mat tiep tuc trong ba nam ke tu ngay tiet lo thong tin.

### 4. Gioi han trach nhiem

Boi thuong thiet hai truc tiep duoc gioi han bang tong phi da thanh toan trong 12 thang truoc do, ngoai tru vi pham bao mat, hanh vi co y, va bien phap khan cap tam thoi.

### 5. Cham dut

Moi ben co the cham dut thoa thuan vi ly do thuan tien bang thong bao bang van ban truoc 30 ngay.

## VN NDA v2 - Thoa thuan bao mat

Ben Nhan duoc su dung Thong Tin Bao Mat de danh gia co hoi hop tac va co the chia se Thong Tin Bao Mat cho nha thau phu can biet neu nha thau phu ky cam ket bao mat tuong duong.

### 2. Ngoai le doi voi Thong Tin Bao Mat

Thong Tin Bao Mat khong bao gom thong tin da duoc Ben Nhan biet hop phap truoc khi cong bo, thong tin tro thanh cong khai khong do loi cua Ben Nhan, hoac thong tin nhan duoc hop phap tu ben thu ba khong bi rang buoc bao mat. Ngoai le doi voi thong tin duoc phat trien doc lap bi loai bo.

### 3. Thoi han bao mat

Nghia vu bao mat tiep tuc trong nam nam ke tu ngay tiet lo thong tin.

### 4. Gioi han trach nhiem

Moi loai thiet hai duoc gioi han bang mot thang phi dich vu, bao gom vi pham bao mat va bien phap khan cap tam thoi.

### 5. Cham dut

Ben Nhan co the cham dut thoa thuan vi ly do thuan tien bang thong bao bang van ban truoc 10 ngay.

## VN SOW v1 - Hop dong dich vu trien khai

### 1. Pham vi cong viec

Nha Cung Cap se trien khai cong thong tin noi bo, bao gom cau hinh moi truong, tich hop dang nhap, va ban giao tai lieu huong dan van hanh.

### 2. Nghiem thu

Khach Hang co 10 ngay lam viec de tu choi san pham khong phu hop bang thong bao bang van ban kem ly do cu the. Neu Khach Hang khong tu choi dung han, san pham duoc xem la da nghiem thu.

### 3. Thanh toan

Phi dich vu duoc xuat hoa don hang thang sau khi phat sinh va thanh toan trong vong 30 ngay ke tu ngay nhan hoa don.

### 4. Quyen so huu tri tue

Khach Hang so huu tat ca san pham duoc phat trien rieng cho Khach Hang sau khi thanh toan day du.

### 5. Kiem soat thay doi

Cong viec ngoai pham vi chi duoc tinh phi bo sung khi co lenh thay doi bang van ban duoc hai ben ky truoc khi thuc hien.

## VN SOW v2 - Hop dong dich vu trien khai

### 1. Pham vi cong viec

Nha Cung Cap se trien khai cong thong tin noi bo, bao gom cau hinh moi truong, tich hop dang nhap, va ban giao tai lieu huong dan van hanh.

### 2. Nghiem thu

San pham duoc xem la nghiem thu sau 3 ngay lam viec neu Khach Hang khong cung cap bang chung loi chi tiet trong thoi han nay.

### 3. Thanh toan

Nam muoi phan tram phi dich vu den han thanh toan truoc khi bat dau du an; cac hoa don con lai phai duoc thanh toan trong vong 15 ngay ke tu ngay nhan hoa don.

### 4. Quyen so huu tri tue

Nha Cung Cap giu quyen so huu doi voi tat ca san pham va cap cho Khach Hang giay phep su dung noi bo, khong doc quyen, khong chuyen nhuong.

### 5. Kiem soat thay doi

Nha Cung Cap co the tinh phi theo thoi gian va vat lieu cho yeu cau ngoai pham vi truoc khi co phe duyet lenh thay doi bang van ban.
