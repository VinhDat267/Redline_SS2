## NDA v1 - Mutual Confidentiality Agreement

### 1. Confidential Information

Confidential Information means non-public business, technical, financial, pricing, product, customer, and operational information disclosed by either party.

### 2. Exclusions

Confidential Information excludes information that is already known by the receiving party, becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, is independently developed without use of Confidential Information, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

### 3. Confidentiality Term

Confidentiality obligations continue for three years after disclosure.

### 4. Limitation of Liability

Direct damages are capped at fees paid in the prior 12 months, excluding confidentiality breach, willful misconduct, and injunctive relief.

### 5. Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience with 30 days written notice.

### 6. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of New York, without regard to conflicts-of-law rules.

## NDA v2 - Mutual Confidentiality Agreement

### 1. Confidential Information

Confidential Information means non-public business, technical, financial, pricing, product, customer, operational, and affiliate information disclosed by either party.

### 2. Exclusions

Confidential Information excludes information that is already known by the receiving party, becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, or is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. The independently developed exclusion is removed.

### 3. Confidentiality Term

Confidentiality obligations continue for five years after disclosure.

### 4. Limitation of Liability

All damages are capped at one month of fees, including confidentiality breach and equitable remedies.

### 5. Termination

Recipient may terminate for convenience with 10 days written notice.

### 6. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of New York, without regard to conflicts-of-law rules.

## SOW v1 - Implementation Statement of Work

### 1. Deliverables

Vendor will provide a requirements workshop, clickable prototype, API integration guide, and deployment checklist.

### 2. Acceptance

Customer has 10 business days to reject non-conforming deliverables with written reasons. Deliverables are accepted when Customer approves in writing or when the review period expires without rejection.

### 3. Payment

Fees are invoiced monthly in arrears and payable 30 days after invoice.

### 4. IP Ownership

Customer owns all custom deliverables upon full payment. Vendor retains ownership of pre-existing tools and background technology.

### 5. Change Control

Out-of-scope work requires a written change order signed by both parties before extra fees apply.

## SOW v2 - Implementation Statement of Work

### 1. Deliverables

Vendor will provide a requirements workshop, clickable prototype, API integration guide, and deployment checklist.

### 2. Acceptance

Deliverables are deemed accepted after 3 business days unless Customer provides detailed defect evidence.

### 3. Payment

Fifty percent of fees are due upfront and remaining invoices are payable 15 days after invoice.

### 4. IP Ownership

Vendor retains ownership of all deliverables and grants Customer a non-exclusive internal-use license.

### 5. Change Control

Vendor may charge time-and-materials fees for out-of-scope requests before written change-order approval.
